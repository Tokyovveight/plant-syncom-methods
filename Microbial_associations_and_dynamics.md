# Methods for inferring microbial associations and modelling community dynamics

Inference estimators, comparison tools and analysis workflows are listed separately. An edge, centrality or driver score alone does not establish cooperation, causation or a keystone role.

| Approach | Data and question | Use for candidate nomination | Main limitation | Validation needed | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pearson / Spearman / Kendall | Linear or rank-based marginal association; prefer measured absolute abundances. | Explore candidate pairs. | Rank correlation does not remove compositionality or environmental confounding. | Test recovered pairs or communities in the recipient. | Friedman and Alm, 2012 |
| SparCC / FastSpar | Approximate correlations of log-transformed latent abundances from compositions; predominantly sparse-correlation assumption. | Nominate covarying pairs and modules. | Marginal associations; zero handling and model assumptions matter. FastSpar implements the same estimand faster. | Recover matching isolates and test context-dependent pair or community effects. | Friedman and Alm, 2012; Watts et al., 2019 |
| Log-ratio proportionality | Stability of abundance ratios across samples. | Nominate taxa maintaining reproducible ratios. | Distinct from absolute correlation or conditional dependence; does not establish cooperation. | Test ratio stability and function after reconstruction. | Lovell et al., 2015 |
| SPIEC-EASI | Sparse undirected conditional associations after compositional transformation. | Nominate parsimonious neighborhoods while reducing indirect links involving observed taxa. | Conditional on observed variables and model assumptions; an edge does not establish ecological interaction. | Use targeted dropout/add-back and quantify member responses. | Kurtz et al., 2015 |
| SPRING | Zero-rich quantitative abundances; rank-based latent correlations and sparse conditional associations. Relative-data extension needs a suitable transform. | Analyze sparse quantitative microbiome measurements. | Depends on latent-model assumptions and quantitative measurements. The relative-data mclr extension does not itself remove compositional effects. | Validate nominated roles by perturbation with absolute member measurements. | Yoon et al., 2019 |
| FlashWeave | Conditional-independence inference from heterogeneous abundance data with optional metadata and structural-zero-aware mode. | Explore large surveys across sites or genotypes while including measured confounders. | Unknown confounding remains; small datasets can lose power; plant-system performance requires evaluation. | Replicate nominated relationships across recipient contexts and test reconstructed communities. | Tackmann et al., 2019 |
| Temporal models: gLV / MDSINE2 | Longitudinal abundance trajectories and perturbation metadata; MDSINE2 uses counts plus total concentrations. Model-dependent directional effects. | Predict responses to removal, invasion or perturbation. | Requires identifiable dynamics and adequate temporal sampling; model coefficients do not identify biochemical mechanisms. | Run prospective perturbations and compare predicted and observed trajectories and plant effects. | Gibson et al., 2025 |
| CoNet (network-inference workflow) | Combination of association measures and resampling procedures. | Explore consensus and sensitivity. | Agreement between measures is not independent biological validation. | Test prioritized relationships experimentally. | Faust and Raes, 2016 |
| NetCoMi (workflow) | Construction, analysis and comparison workflow incorporating existing estimators. | Compare networks reproducibly. | Report the underlying inference estimator; it is not a single new edge estimand. | Follow up reproducible differential roles with community perturbation. | Peschel et al., 2021 |
| NetShift (comparison) | Neighborhood rewiring and role changes between state-specific networks. | Nominate taxa associated with network reorganization. | Driver score does not establish causal direction or a keystone role. | Use source/state-controlled dropout and add-back experiments. | Kuntal et al., 2019 |
| PhONA (phenotype integration) | Integration of phenotype-associated taxa and microbial network neighborhoods. | Nominate phenotype-relevant candidate assemblages. | Prediction or association does not validate an assembled SynCom. | Reconstruct proposed assemblages and measure plant and member outcomes. | Poudel et al., 2023 |
